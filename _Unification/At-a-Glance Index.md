## How to read the document from here

The 10 process area sections (§3.01 through §3.10) follow a single template: how each company approaches the area today, where they align, where they differ (numbered divergences with explicit decisions), cross-area dependencies, recommendation summary, and the leadership-team decisions table with reconciling math.

Process areas are walked **linearly in document order**, not reordered by complexity. A complexity-based ordering tempts readers to skip ahead; a linear order matches how configuration is done and how the working session will run. Low-density areas may take 10 minutes of session time; high-density areas may take an hour.

Cross-references between sections are explicit. Where a decision from one area surfaces in another, it is flagged in the receiving section's Cross-Area Dependencies table and resolved by reading the home section. The full Cross-Area Decisions Index at `11 - Cross-Area Decisions Index.md` is the single-source-of-truth for these threads.

The on-site working session output is a Merged BRD per area, drafted within 10 business days of the session and circulated for sign-off. Configuration resumes from the merged BRDs in June.
